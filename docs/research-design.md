# Proposed Research Design

## Status

This is a design for future study. No recruitment, fieldwork, assessment, intervention, or outcome tracking has begun. The design does not create authority to certify or qualify any firm.

## Study purpose

The proposed study will examine whether small manufacturers and suppliers can identify, document, and maintain the qualifications required for defined aerospace and advanced manufacturing opportunities in the New Orleans East and Michoud corridor. It will measure requirement coverage and evidence readiness at the firm-opportunity pair level.

## Unit of analysis

The primary unit of analysis will be a firm-opportunity pair. A single firm may face different requirements for different buyers, products, contracts, or supply-chain tiers. This structure prevents a general label such as “supplier ready” from replacing opportunity-specific evidence.

## Proposed assessment domains

| Domain | Proposed assessment focus |
|---|---|
| Opportunity definition | Buyer, supply-chain tier, product or service, applicable solicitation or qualification route |
| Requirement identification | Completeness and source traceability of applicable obligations |
| Quality management | Controlled procedures, responsibilities, records, corrective action, and internal review |
| Technical process | Equipment, process control, work instructions, validation, and special-process dependencies |
| Inspection and measurement | Inspection planning, calibration, metrology, acceptance criteria, and retained results |
| Document control | Versioning, approval, distribution, retention, and retrieval |
| Supply-chain control | Supplier qualification, purchasing controls, provenance, and lower-tier evidence |
| Cybersecurity and information handling | Applicable contractual controls, system boundaries, evidence, and renewal |
| Delivery and continuity | Capacity evidence, scheduling, risk controls, and contingency planning |
| Registrations and representations | Current external registrations and opportunity-specific representations |
| Workforce evidence | Role qualifications, training records, authorizations, and continuity risks |
| Financial and administrative readiness | Cost accounting, invoicing, insurance, bonding, and other applicable controls |

Domains will be included only when supported by the target opportunity. The instrument will record `N/A` with a reason when a domain or requirement does not apply.

## Proposed sampling approach

The study will use purposive, staged sampling after feasibility and ethics review. Candidate firms will be screened for location, size, relevant technical capability, and interest in a defined opportunity class. Recruitment will avoid claims that participation creates buyer access, certification, contract preference, or award probability.

The initial sample size remains unset. It will depend on accessible firms, the number of defined opportunity classes, assessment effort, data-sharing constraints, and the minimum observations required for the selected analysis. Any future sample target will be documented before recruitment.

## Proposed instruments

| Instrument | Function |
|---|---|
| Eligibility screener | Records firm and opportunity inclusion criteria |
| Requirement-source register | Maps each obligation to an authoritative source |
| Evidence-readiness matrix | Records presence, currency, traceability, ownership, and reviewer status |
| Structured interview guide | Captures how requirements are found, interpreted, implemented, and maintained |
| Document-control audit | Tests version, approval, retention, retrieval, and cross-reference controls |
| Qualification lifecycle log | Tracks preparation, submission, review, correction, acceptance, and expiry |
| Burden log | Records preparation time, external dependencies, fees, repeat work, and waiting time |
| Referral register | Records assistance needs, referrals, uptake, and bounded outcomes |
| Verification register | Separates verified facts, partial verification, and claims not located in public records |

Draft instruments will be piloted before formal data collection. Definitions, scoring rules, and required evidence will be version controlled.

## Proposed measures

### Baseline measures

- Applicable requirements identified
- Evidence items present and retrievable
- Evidence items current at assessment
- Requirements linked to a source and owner
- Time-limited approvals with recorded expiry dates
- Gaps by assessment domain and lifecycle stage

### Process measures

- Time from requirement identification to completed evidence
- Time waiting on external review or dependency
- Number of correction or resubmission cycles
- Assistance referrals issued and accepted
- Evidence items completed, renewed, or withdrawn
- Requirement changes and resulting rework

### Outcome measures

- Change in verified evidence coverage
- Completion of opportunity-specific qualification milestones
- Buyer or authorized-reviewer acceptance where observable
- Maintenance of time-limited evidence at follow-up
- Participation outcomes that can be verified through an authoritative source

The design will report numerators, denominators, observation dates, and eligibility rules for each rate. A completed internal checklist will not be treated as external qualification.

## Proposed analysis

Descriptive analysis will summarize requirement counts, evidence coverage, lifecycle states, burden, and timing by domain and opportunity class. Small samples will be reported with counts and distributions instead of unsupported population estimates.

Within-firm change may be examined when the same instrument and definitions are used at baseline and follow-up. Causal claims will require a comparison or phased design that addresses selection, project mix, and concurrent assistance.

## Data governance

Public reporting will remove firm names, personnel names, proprietary processes, buyer-sensitive data, contract identifiers, and correspondence. Source artifacts will remain in controlled storage. Publication rules, consent, retention, and access will be defined before fieldwork.

The public verification register will cite offices and authoritative sources. It will not reproduce private messages or characterize any individual's conduct.
