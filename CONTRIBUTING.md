# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they improve the public research design, evidence discipline, or literature coverage without introducing private or unsupported material.

## Appropriate contributions

- Peer-reviewed literature relevant to supplier readiness, qualification burden, documentation burden, rework, requirements volatility, submittal/RFI processing, or small-firm compliance cost
- Corrections to public-source claims or source grading
- Improvements to measurement definitions, sampling, validation, or causal design
- Better terminology grounded in established research
- Reproducibility improvements to the literature-review or verification process

## Do not submit

- Private correspondence, screenshots, personal contact information, internal Drive links, or unpublished operational records
- Proprietary firm data, buyer-sensitive records, contract identifiers, or assessment artifacts
- Claims that ORB certifies, qualifies, funds, represents, or places firms into programs
- Claims that the proposed gap is novel before the literature review establishes it
- Unsupported population estimates, causal claims, or precise figures without a verifiable source

## Evidence discipline

Quantitative claims should retain the source, date, population, numerator/denominator where applicable, and explicit qualifiers. A `Not located` finding is not proof that a record does not exist.

Literature suggestions should include a DOI or stable citation when available and indicate which construct, method, or proposed gap they bear on.

## Pull requests

Keep pull requests focused and explain:

1. What changed.
2. Why it changed.
3. What source or research principle supports it.
4. Whether it changes a claim, construct, proposed instrument, or limitation.

By contributing, you agree that accepted documentation contributions may be distributed under the repository's CC BY 4.0 license.
